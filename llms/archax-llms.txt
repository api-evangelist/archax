# Archax

> Archax is a group of regulated digital asset companies operating across the UK, EU, and US. archax.com is the group's corporate website, operated by Archax Holdings Ltd (parent).

## Group Structure

- [archax.com](https://archax.com) - Archax Group. Brand story, capabilities, entity directory.
- [uk.archax.com](https://uk.archax.com) - Archax Limited. FCA authorised (FRN 838656). Exchange, custody, tokenisation, brokerage.
- [eu.archax.com](https://eu.archax.com) - Archax Markets Europe, S.V., S.A. CNMV authorised. MiFID II passporting.
- [us.archax.com](https://us.archax.com) - Archax Markets LLC. FINRA registered. Digital securities brokerage.

## Core Capabilities

- **Regulated Venues:** Multilateral Trading Facilities (MTFs) in the UK and EU for institutional trading of digital and tokenised assets
- **Tokenisation:** Beneficial ownership tokenisation of real-world assets (bonds, funds, equities) on public and permissioned blockchain networks
- **Institutional Custody:** Insolvency-remote segregated safekeeping via Northern Trust sub-custody and Fireblocks technology
- **Brokerage & OTC:** Principal and agency execution for digital assets and tokenised securities; clearing via tZero in the US

## Regulatory Credentials

- Archax Limited: FCA authorised cryptoasset business and MTF operator (FRN 838656)
- Archax Markets Europe, S.V., S.A.: CNMV-authorised MiFID II investment firm (Spain)
- Archax Markets LLC: FINRA-registered introducing broker-dealer (US)

## What archax.com Is NOT

- archax.com does not offer financial services directly or accept client registrations
- archax.com is operated by Archax Holdings Ltd, which is not itself regulated by the FCA, CNMV, or FINRA
- Specific products and services are offered only through the regulated entity in each jurisdiction
- archax.com does not contain financial promotions, yields, returns, or investment advice

## Key Partnerships

- **Northern Trust:** Sub-custody provider for institutional asset safekeeping
- **Fireblocks:** MPC key management technology for digital asset custody
- **tZero:** Clearing firm for US digital securities brokerage (Archax Markets LLC)
